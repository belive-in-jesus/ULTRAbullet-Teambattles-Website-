HOW IT IS CALCULATED

How the score works

Each battle result is changed to a score from 0 to 100. First place gets 100, last place gets 0, and the other places fall between them.

Average result40%
Consistency25%
Recent results · last 10 battles20%
Participation · up to 50 battles15%

The consistency score is higher when results stay similar from battle to battle. It starts at 100 and falls as results vary more. The participation score reaches its full value after 50 recorded battles. The PI is a statistic, not an official Lichess rating.
