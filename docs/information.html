Understand the leaderboard.

This page explains which battles count, how the rankings work and what the stats mean. We want every result to be clear and easy to check.

01 / Eligibility

Which battles count?

We count finished, numbered ULTRAbullet Team Battles created by the tournament organiser. A battle must use the regular title format and have a finished status on Lichess.

  • Regular numbered Team Battles are included.
  • Unnumbered and special events are excluded.
  • Alliance, qualification, Christmas, Daily Arena and restarted events do not add points to the regular rankings.
02 / Points

How are points counted?

Points come from the results published on Lichess. Team rankings use team points; player rankings use each player's points. All counted battles add to the all-time totals.

  • Battles played: eligible tournaments with a recorded result.
  • Average points: points divided by recorded battles played.
  • Podiums: finishes in the top three; first places are counted separately.
03 / Ranking periods

All-time, monthly and seasonal rankings

You can view the same results over three time periods. Months use the UTC calendar. Seasons use the official battle numbers, even when some results are missing.

All-timeEvery numbered battle in the archive.
MonthlyBattles grouped by month using UTC time.
SeasonalEach season covers 50 battle numbers: Season 1 = 1–50, Season 2 = 51–100, and so on.
Important: A missing battle number does not move later battles into another season. The gap stays in its original place.
04 / Updates

When is the data refreshed?

The data is updated automatically twice a day, at 06:15 and 18:15 Berlin time. The home page shows when the latest successful update took place.

The site is a public, static frontend. No Lichess API token is placed in the website files or exposed to visitors.

05 / Data quality

How should gaps be understood?

We can only show battles that we can find and whose results we can read. The current season view shows missing battle numbers when known. A missing result does not mean that a player or team scored zero points.

06 / Profile analytics

How should profile charts be read?

Player and team profiles show points over time, finish places, monthly points, battle activity and season results. You can choose a time period, and the score chart can also show only recent battles.

  • Podium rate: top-three finishes divided by recorded finishes with a known placement.
  • Monthly points: points earned in eligible battles started during that UTC month.
  • Activity calendar: each square shows the number of recorded battles entered on that UTC date. It does not count days without a tournament as missed results.
  • Season analytics: fixed official blocks of 50 numbered battles. A competitor's number of battles entered may be lower than the season's total.
Data note: Missing results are not counted as zero points. Stats use only the results that are available in the archive.
07 / Battle reports & rank history

Explore individual battles and ranking development

Open Overview → Battle history and select a battle title to view its compact report. The report shows the recorded team and individual standings, participant count and a direct link to the original Lichess tournament. Detailed results are loaded only when a report is opened.

On a player or team profile, the Performance over time chart can switch between Score and All-time rank. Rank history shows the cumulative overall position at the last available numbered battle in each fixed 50-battle season. It is not the placement in an individual tournament; lower rank numbers indicate a higher position.

Data note: Battle reports and rank history are created during a successful data update. A new report may not appear until the next update has finished.
08 / Project

About this leaderboard

This is an independent community website for recurring Lichess UltraBullet Team Battles. It brings results together in one searchable archive. It is not an official Lichess rating or title system.

Every team and player has a profile page with results, stats, recent form and battle history. Open a team or player from a leaderboard or the Hall of Fame to view its profile.

The small symbol beside each name uses its first letter. It is only for display; names and Lichess links stay the same.

The results script is based on DarkOnCrack's Gift For Nathanael, used with permission. Tournament play and results remain on Lichess.org.
