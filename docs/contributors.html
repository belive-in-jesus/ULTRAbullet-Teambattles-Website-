How to read this ranking

Contributor records are taken from the players listed in each official Lichess team-battle standing. A player’s listed score is added to their career total, and duplicate snapshots for the same player and battle are counted only once. This is a historical contribution snapshot, not a complete team-membership register; players may have represented different teams in different battles.

Only regular numbered UltraBullet Team Battles are included. Special and unnumbered events remain outside this ranking.
